What Is Contract Freeze Drying?

Contract freeze drying means a company freeze dries your product for you instead of you buying and running a freeze dryer. You bring the product or formulation, agree on the scope, and the work is carried out on equipment you do not have to own.

Businesses choose this route when they are testing a new product, when volume does not yet justify a capital purchase, or when they want to add freeze-dried items without building the in-house skills and floor space to run a dryer.
